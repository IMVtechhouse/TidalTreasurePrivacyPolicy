1 Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Tidal Treasure: Coin Sort & Merge ("the Game", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and published by IMVTechHouse. We respect your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, the services we use for in-game advertising and analytics, and how your privacy rights are safeguarded when you enjoy our game.

2 Information We Collect & How We Use It

Tidal Treasure: Coin Sort & Merge does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal identity profiles (such as real name, phone number, physical address, or contact book access). You can play the full campaign and endless modes completely offline or online without registering.

Device & Game State Data (Stored Locally): Your coins, skins, level progress, sound preferences, and high scores are stored locally on your device via encrypted local storage (Hive). We do not transmit or sell your game state to external databases.

To deliver free-to-play features, rewarded bonuses, and game stability, we integrate industry-standard SDKs that collect limited technical telemetry:

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID) utilized by Google AdMob to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, track ad performance, and prevent fraud.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, locale, and mobile network operator.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: App crashes, frame metrics, and load times to continuously optimize high-refresh-rate (120/165 Hz) gameplay and resolve bugs.

4 Data Retention & Security

All in-game progress is preserved locally on your physical device. We utilize secure HTTPS encryption for all external network communications conducted by third-party SDKs.

If you uninstall the game or clear app storage through your device settings, your local progress and preferences will be permanently deleted from your device.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application is designed for a general audience and is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt steps to remove such information.

6 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area or California), you may have statutory rights regarding your personal data:

  • Opting Out of Targeted Advertising: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your Android device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Right of Access & Deletion: Because all game save data is stored locally on your device, you have immediate and complete control to delete your data at any time by clearing app cache/storage or uninstalling the app.
